### SIM on FHIR

How the **Santeon Informatie Model (SIM)** maps to FHIR. The guide is general; it is not tied to one disease area.

#### Scope

* **SIM logical models**, including mappings to the source CSVs, the Nictiz GBB and FHIR ([overview](CLMs.md)).
* **FHIR profiles** for the SIM building blocks, each with the SIM provenance per element and, where a matching profile exists, a technical comparison with Nictiz nl-core ([overview](profiles.md)).
* **Datasets**, one per disease area (currently IBD): the cohort, the KPI definitions and the export request for each, derived from [KPI definitions](kpi-definitions.md).
* **Indicators** that use the datasets.
* **De-identification** of exports.
* **Source**: the Santeon Informatie Model.
